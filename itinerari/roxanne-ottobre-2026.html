TUE20

Campo Tartano & the Ponte nel Cielo

On foot · a few minutes from the house
  • The "scary bridge": the Ponte nel Cielo, one of the highest suspension bridges in Europe, a few minutes' walk from home. Usually open daytime, about 9:30–16:30.
  • Afternoon walk on the valley trails (free) and rest after the long flight.

Cost: bridge ticket ≈ €5–8 per person, paid on site. Everything else free.

WED21

Lake Como by bus, train & boat

Bus → Morbegno · train 40 min → Varenna · boats
  • Bus (or minibus shuttle) to Morbegno station, regional train to Varenna.
  • Boat loop Varenna → Bellagio → Menaggio → Varenna (15–20 min per crossing).
  • Free: Varenna's lakeside walk, Bellagio's stepped lanes and lake views, Menaggio's waterfront.

Cost: ≈ €30 per person (bus, train and 3 boat rides). Bring packed lunches to save.

THU22

Switzerland: St. Moritz & the Bernina line

Minibus 1 h 15 → Tirano · red train 2 h 30 → St. Moritz
  • Minibus to Tirano, then the red Bernina train (the normal regional train, same famous route, cheaper than the "Bernina Express") over the glaciers to St. Moritz.
  • Free: walk around the lake of St. Moritz and the town centre.
  • The minibus meets you in St. Moritz and drives you home over the Maloja Pass (≈ 2 h), so you only pay the train one way.
  • Passports needed. Switzerland uses Swiss francs; cards work everywhere.

Cost: train ≈ €33 per person one way (child fares are lower) + minibus.

Venice

Minibus ≈ 3 h 45 each way · long day

About Rome

Rome is about 6 hours each way from the house (bus, train to Milan, then the high-speed train), so it can't be a day trip with the kids. Two ways to include it:

  • End the trip in Rome: on 28 Oct take the high-speed train from Milan (≈ 3 h), sleep one or two nights in Rome and fly home from Rome.
  • Skip it this year and keep Rome for next year's trip with the academies.

High-speed tickets Milan–Rome are cheaper booked early (≈ €30–60 per person each way).
