**Our fee**

Our fee is **15% of the nightly rate, calculated net of the Airbnb fee**, plus VAT. Cleaning fees and tourist tax are not included in the calculation.

**The advantage that makes the difference: rental tax on the net amount**

With our model the listing is managed by BB Hospitality in your name and on your behalf, and the Italian flat-rate rental tax (cedolare secca) applies **only to what you actually keep**, not to everything the guest pays. The Airbnb fee, cleaning and our fee are no longer taxed.

An example: for a two-night booking at €200 per night with €80 cleaning (Airbnb fee 15.5% + VAT, rental tax 21%):

- the Airbnb fee is €90.77 and cleaning €80;
- our fee is €48.65, plus €10.70 VAT;
- the rental tax is calculated on €249.88 instead of €480, so it drops from €100.80 to €52.47;
- you keep **€197.41 net** instead of **€159.78**.

That is **€37 more per booking**, around **€1,880 more per year** over 100 nights. We recommend checking these figures with your accountant.
